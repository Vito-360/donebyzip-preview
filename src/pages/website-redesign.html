Signs your site needs a redesign

  • It loads slowly on a phone.
  • Visitors can’t tell where to click to contact you.
  • It looks dated, so you look closed.
  • It doesn’t show up on Google when people search for your service and town.

What we check

  • Google profile: does it send people to your site.
  • Your site: does it show in a few seconds that you’re the right choice.
  • The path to an enquiry: can a visitor get in touch or book without effort.

Fix it or rebuild?

If the foundation is sound, we fix what’s holding it back. If it isn’t, a new site is often cheaper than patching the old one. Either way, you decide, based on written findings.

Questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on four things: how many pages, whether you need bookings or an enquiry form, who writes the copy, and how many languages. You get the exact amount after the free check, before any work starts.

Will I lose my Google rankings?

We take care of that. Old addresses are redirected to the new ones, so neither Google nor visitors land on an empty page.

Do I keep my domain?

Yes. The domain is yours, and at launch you get full ownership: files, accounts, access to everything.
