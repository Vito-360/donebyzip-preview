How to show Google ads only in your town and during opening hours

9 November 2026 · 2 min read · zip

If you change nothing, your ads can show to people outside your area and at three in the morning. Four settings fix that.

1. Location: your town or a radius

In the campaign settings, choose your town or a radius around your address (at least 1 km). Google advises targeting the places where your customers are, which isn’t always the same place your business is.

2. “Presence”, not “Presence or interest”

The default setting also shows ads to people who have only shown interest in your area, even if they are somewhere else. For a local business, choose “Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations”. Google says your ads then show only to people who are likely to be in that area.

3. Ad schedule

By default, campaigns show ads all day. In the ad schedule, set the days and hours when you’re open and can answer an enquiry. The schedule follows your account’s time zone, which is set when the account is created and can’t be changed afterwards, so check that it is Zagreb.

4. The call button only when someone answers

If you show your phone number with the ads, you can give it days and hours too, for example only when someone is there to answer. A call nobody picks up is a paid click and a lost customer.

Quick check

  • Location: your town or a radius around your address.
  • Location option: “Presence”.
  • Ad schedule: your opening hours.
  • Account time zone: Zagreb.
  • Phone number: only during the hours someone answers.
