Where to send people from Instagram: a bio link that brings enquiries

9 November 2026 · 2 min read · zip

Someone who taps the link in your bio is already interested. Don’t make them search all over again.

Your bio has room for 150 characters and a website link, both added under Edit profile. That link is the shortest way from your profile to an enquiry, so make it count.

Not the home page, but a page for enquiries

A home page often offers ten different things. Send the bio link to a page that answers four questions: what you do, where, roughly how much it costs and how to get in touch, with a call or message button at the top.

WhatsApp with a ready message

WhatsApp lets you make a link that opens a chat with you and a message already typed, for example “Hi, I’d like to book…”. The customer only taps Send. The link is wa.me/ followed by your number in international format, without the + sign, the leading zero or spaces.

Use the buttons on your profile too

A professional account can show a Contact button with your phone number, email and directions. With partners such as Booksy or Fresha you can add a booking button, but only one such button shows on your profile at a time.

A bio that leads to the next step

Use one line for what you do and where, and one for what the reader should do next. An example for a made-up hair salon: “Haircuts and colour · Trešnjevka, Zagreb / Book through the link below”. If you use a page with several links, keep it to two or three, with enquiries first.
