How to answer a message or comment so it turns into an enquiry

9 November 2026 · 2 min read · zip

Someone asked “How much?” under your post. How you answer decides whether they book or keep scrolling.

Answer quickly

Speed shows in public too. Facebook automatically gives Pages the “Very responsive” badge when they answer at least 90% of messages within 15 minutes on average. Automatic replies and away messages don’t count.

Answer the question, not just “check your messages”

Under a comment, answer briefly and to the point, and move the details to messages. “We’ve sent you a message” with no answer also puts off everyone else who reads it. Better, for a made-up pilates studio: “Group classes start at €12. We’ve sent you this week’s times in a message.”

Don’t ask people to post their phone number in a comment, where everyone can see it. Ask them to send it in a message.

End with one step

End every answer with one question or one step: “Does Thursday at 6 pm work for you?” or “You can book here: …”.

Saved answers, but not robotic

Business and creator accounts on Instagram can set up to four frequently asked questions with an automatic answer, shown when someone messages you. Meta Business Suite also has automated responses, such as an instant reply and an away message. Use them for prices, hours and your address, and answer everything else yourself.
