It depends on four things: how many pages, whether you need bookings or an enquiry form, who writes the copy, and how many languages. You get the exact amount after the free check, before any work starts.

How long does it take?

A simple site takes 1–2 weeks. Larger projects get their timeline with the proposal.

Do I have to write the copy?

No. We can write it for you, in Croatian or English.
