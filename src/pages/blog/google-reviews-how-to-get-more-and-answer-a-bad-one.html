Google reviews: how to get more and how to answer a bad one

9 November 2026 · 2 min read · zip

Reviews are what people read before they call. You can’t buy good ones or delete bad ones, but you can ask for more and answer the ones that hurt.

Google says the number of reviews and your rating are part of how well-known your business is, one of the three things that decide local results. More reviews and good ratings can help you show up higher.

Ask at the right moment

Most happy customers never leave a review because nobody asked them. Ask every customer when the job is done: after the haircut, when you hand over the keys, when the repair works. Don’t pick only the ones you think will write something nice.

Make it one tap

In your Google Business Profile you can create a link and a QR code that open the review form directly. Google suggests putting it on receipts, in thank-you emails, at the end of a chat or printed in your shop.

What you mustn’t do

  • Offer a discount, a gift or anything else in exchange for a review.
  • Ask only the customers you expect to be happy, or discourage bad reviews.
  • Write reviews yourself or ask friends who were never customers.

Google removes such reviews and can restrict or close the account behind them.

Answering a bad review

You can’t delete a bad review because you disagree with it. You can report it only if it breaks Google’s rules, for example if it’s spam or doesn’t describe a real experience.

Your reply is read by every future customer, so write for them:

  1. Thank them and say you’re sorry about the experience, without arguing.
  2. If there is something to explain, explain it briefly and without personal details.
  3. Offer to sort it out by phone or email.
  4. Keep it short and calm. Google also recommends short, professional replies.

An example for a made-up hair salon in Zagreb: “Thank you for telling us. We’re sorry the colour wasn’t what you expected. Please call us on … and we’ll find a time to look at it together.”

Reply to good reviews too, briefly and in your own words. The other profile mistakes that cost enquiries are in 7 Google profile mistakes that stop customers finding you.
