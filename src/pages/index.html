What the free check looks like

Example: a made-up hair salon, not a client.

  1. Google listing

    The listing has no link to the website. People who want more than a phone number have nowhere to go.

    Fix: Add the website link and point it to a page with a tappable phone number.

  2. Website

    The first screen says ‘Quality since 2005’. It doesn’t say what the salon does or where, so visitors can’t tell they’re in the right place.

    Fix: First line: what, where, for whom. Haircuts and colouring, Trešnjevka, Zagreb.

  3. Path to an enquiry

    The phone number can’t be tapped on a phone. That’s the step where most people give up.

    Fix: One button: book or message on WhatsApp.
