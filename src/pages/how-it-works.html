How it works

From the free check to a site that’s yours. Every step agreed with you.

01

The free check

  • Send your website address or your business name on WhatsApp. That’s all we need.
  • We look at three things:
    • Your Google listing: the category, the link to your site, opening hours, photos and replies to reviews.
    • Your website: whether the first screen says what you do, where and for whom; how fast it is on a phone; whether the phone number can be tapped.
    • The path to an enquiry: how many steps it takes to call, book or send a message, and whether the form actually arrives.
  • You get a reply within 24 hours. The three findings, in writing and each with a fix, usually follow within the next 12 hours.
  • No obligation and no sales call. The findings are yours, whether you work with us or not.
02

A conversation and a demo

  • If you want us to fix what we found, we talk through what you need: on a video call, or in person if you’re in or near Zagreb.
  • Then you get a demo version of your new site, so you see what you’d get before you decide.
03

A proposal in writing

  • Scope, timeline and price, in writing, before any work starts. What’s included and what costs extra, line by line.
  • Payment: half when you go ahead, half at launch.
  • You decide. Nothing starts until you agree.
04

Build

  • A simple site takes 1–2 weeks.
  • What we need from you: your logo if you have one, photos (or we bring in a photographer), access to your Google profile and domain, and one person who gives feedback.
  • We write the copy, in Croatian or English.
  • You see the site at every stage. Two to three rounds of changes are included; larger projects get more.
05

Launch and handover

  • The site goes live on the domain you choose (.hr, .com or .eu), linked to your Google profile, with analytics so you see what it brings in.
  • Redesign: old addresses are redirected to the new ones, so neither Google nor visitors land on an empty page.
  • Everything is yours: files, accounts and access.
06

After launch

  • With monthly care, we fix any error and make small changes for you. Bigger additions, like a blog, start with a new conversation and a proposal.
  • Without care, the site is yours to edit and look after.
  • Add-ons when you need them: be found (local SEO, Google and Meta ads) and save time (AI assistant, booking reminders, review requests).
