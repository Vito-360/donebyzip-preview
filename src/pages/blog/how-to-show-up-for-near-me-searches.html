How to show up when someone searches “[service] near me”

9 November 2026 · 2 min read · zip

When someone types “pilates near me” or “plumber near me”, Google shows them businesses nearby that match what they need. You can’t change the distance, but you can work on the rest.

Google names three things that decide local results: how well a profile matches the search, how far the business is from the person searching, and how well-known it is. If the search doesn’t name a place, Google works out the distance from what it knows about the person’s location. There is no way to pay for a better place in the local results.

1. A verified, complete Google profile

Your profile has to be verified. Pick the most specific primary category, and add your services, opening hours and the area you cover. Google says complete, accurate information helps it match you to the right searches. The most common gaps are in 7 Google profile mistakes that stop customers finding you.

2. A name without keywords

It’s tempting to call yourself “Pilates Zagreb – best studio in town”. Google’s rules don’t allow it: the name on your profile has to be the one you use in real life, on your sign and your invoices. Extra words can get the profile suspended.

3. Say what and where on your website

Someone who comes to your site from the map should see at once that they’re in the right place: what you do, in which town or neighbourhood, and how to reach you. Keep your name, address and phone exactly the same as on the profile.

4. Reviews

Reviews are part of how Google judges how well-known you are: more reviews and good ratings can help. How to get them without breaking the rules is in Google reviews: how to get more and how to answer a bad one.

Quick check

  • The profile is verified.
  • The primary category is as specific as possible.
  • The name is the same as on your sign, with nothing added.
  • Opening hours and services are filled in.
  • The first line of your site says what you do and where.
  • You ask for reviews and reply to them.
